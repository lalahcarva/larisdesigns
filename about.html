Hello! My name is Larissa Carvalho.

I am a Graphic Designer and Illustrator.

I’m a passionate graphic designer based in Toronto, ON, with a love for all things creative. For me, design isn’t just a job, it’s part of who I am. I have a sharp eye for detail and a real love for aesthetics, and I get so much joy from turning ideas into visuals that truly stand out. Creativity keeps me inspired every day, pushing me to think in new ways, explore fresh ideas, and design work that’s both meaningful and innovative.

Why Design?

Ever since I was young, I knew I wanted a life full of colors, shapes, and endless creativity. To me, design is the bridge between imagination and the real world. It’s a way to tell stories visually, and I think there’s something really magical about that.

What Sets Me Apart?

Over the years, I’ve worked hard to grow my skills and develop a strong sense of how to communicate visually. Things like typography, color, and layout aren’t just design terms to me, they’re the tools I use to bring ideas to life. Every project feels like a chance to tell a story, get creative, and create something that truly connects with people. Whether it’s a friendly interface, a playful illustration, or a beautiful print piece, I put my heart and soul into every project.

My Journey So Far

My design journey has been an exciting mix of experiences, from freelancing to running my own shop where I sold print products featuring my illustrations and working as an in house Graphic Design. I’ve worked with all kinds of clients and projects, helping bring brands to life and exploring new ideas. Along the way, I’ve built skills in production coordination, graphic design, and managing freelance work, all while staying focused on what really matters: creating designs that meet client needs, feel good for the user, and respect budgets and sustainability. Each experience hasenriched my creative journey, presenting new challenges and opportunities for growth.

Beyond Design

When I’m not working on a project, I’m usually looking for inspiration – whether that’s through other artists, a walk in nature, reading a book, watercolor painting, or discovering new coffee shops. I believe the best ideas often come from staying curious, continuously learning and finding inspiration in life experiences.

Let's Create Together

I'm here to bring ideas to life. Let's go together on a journey of creativity, innovation, and design that leaves a lasting impression. Thank you for stopping by, I can't wait to connect and create something amazing together
